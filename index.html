Positions use Lahiri sidereal longitudes computed for 06:00 IST, accurate to about 0.02° — tithi and nakshatra match a drik panchanga on the day. Tithis change during the day, so near a boundary the printed panchanga may differ. For teaching, not for muhurta.

MOON fixed by month + paksha + tithi, so they wander ±3 weeks against Jan–Dec. SUN fixed by the Sun crossing a line (Sankranti), so they sit on nearly the same English date every year. Amanta reckoning (Karnataka / South India).
